Your first visit

Four steps, in this order, every time.

No treatment is booked on a first visit for anything requiring a prescription. This is not us being slow. It is the condition under which this clinic is allowed to operate, and the reason nothing here goes wrong quietly.

Step 01

Consultation and history

Medication, pregnancy, autoimmune conditions, prior treatment and what you actually want changed. Forty five minutes, unrushed.

Step 02

Skin assessment

Fitzpatrick typing under clinic light, sun damage mapping and a note of anything that rules a treatment out for you specifically.

Step 03

Patch test

Required before any laser or peel course. Minimum 48 hours. If your skin reacts, we change the plan rather than the dose.

Step 04

Written plan

Sessions, spacing, cost and what happens if it does not work. You take it home. Nothing is booked in the room under pressure.

If a clinic offers you a prescription treatment before a prescriber has assessed you, that is not efficiency. Ask them which registered practitioner wrote the script.

Questions

The ones people ask in the car park.

Some of these answers are shaped by Australian advertising law rather than by preference. Where that is the case, we say so instead of leaving you wondering.

Why does this site never name the injectable brands?

Because we are not allowed to. In Australia, the products used for anti wrinkle injections and most dermal fillers are prescription only medicines, and advertising prescription only medicines to the public is prohibited under the Therapeutic Goods Act. Any Australian clinic naming brands publicly is breaking that rule. We will discuss product with you in consultation, where it is lawful and where it is actually useful to you.

Why are there no before and after photos?

Advertising of regulated health services is governed by AHPRA and by the national law. Testimonials about regulated health services are not permitted in advertising, and imagery of prescription treatment outcomes sits under the same restrictions. We show you relevant case imagery in the clinic, in person, where it is a clinical discussion rather than an advertisement.

Do I really need a consultation before treatment?

For anything requiring a prescription, yes, and there is no way around it. A prescriber must assess you before a script can be written. The consultation fee is $75 and comes off your treatment in full, so it costs you nothing if you go ahead.

I have deeper skin. Is laser safe for me?

Yes, on the right wavelength and the right settings. Fitzpatrick types IV to VI are treated on 1064 nm Nd:YAG rather than alexandrite, at lower fluence with longer intervals. The patch test matters more here, not less. If a clinic offers you laser without typing your skin first, leave.

What happens if something goes wrong?

You call the clinic number, not a booking app. Elise carries the after hours line for injectable patients for 14 days after treatment. Vascular complications are rare and time critical, and the clinic holds the reversal agent on site for filler. This is a question worth asking anywhere you are treated.

Can I come on my lunch break?

For anti wrinkle injections and a medical grade facial, yes. For filler, take the afternoon. For a peel or needling, book it on a Thursday and give yourself the weekend. The downtime column above is honest, which is why some of it is longer than you would like.

Wordfence, Cloudflare, hosting

On WordPress the standard is Wordfence for the application firewall and malware scanning, Cloudflare in front for DNS, TLS, caching and rate limiting, and a hardened host with daily off site backups and staging. Core, theme and plugin updates get applied on staging and tested before they touch production, because most WordPress incidents arrive through an unpatched plugin rather than a breached password. This page has none of that surface, which is the point of building it this way.

QA before release

Every page ships against the same list. Rendering at 360, 768, 1024 and 1440. Keyboard only navigation through every control. Colour contrast measured rather than eyeballed, which is why the gold on this page shifts to a darker value at small sizes. Forms tested for empty, invalid and duplicate submission. Links and anchors resolved. Schema validated. Console clean. Reduced motion honoured. Anything failing goes back before release, not after a client reports it.

AI in the build

AI does the volume work: first draft copy against a brief, page variants across service and location, schema generation, alt text, internal link mapping and content refresh cycles. It does not decide layout, and it does not get the final word on anything a regulator reads. The compliance decisions on this page were made by a person, then checked twice.
