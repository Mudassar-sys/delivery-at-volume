Pain 02

The reason good people keep leaving.

A fixed hire is a flat line drawn across a demand curve that is anything but flat. In a quiet week you carry cost you are not using. In a spike week the work is late and the client hears about it. Neither is a people problem.

Pay for the peak, not the average.

The grey line is what a single fixed hire absorbs. Every red bar above it is work that went out late. Everything below it is paid for and idle. The green line follows the work instead of the calendar, which is the only arrangement that survives a week like seven.

The only arrangement that survives week seven is one where capacity is bought by the job rather than by the month. Quiet fortnight, you pay for a quiet fortnight. Launch week, the queue still clears on the same day it arrived.

The part worth two minutes

Anti wrinkle and filler products are prescription only medicines. Advertising them to the public is prohibited under the Therapeutic Goods Act, so that page names no brand anywhere. AHPRA does not permit testimonials in advertising for regulated health services, so it carries none, and clinical protocol does the trust work instead.

Every clinic client you take runs into both rules. Most find out after the site is live. Building it in from the start costs nothing and removes a category of problem you should never have to hear about.

Straight answers

The four things worth knowing before a call.

No capability list and no slide deck. These are the answers that actually decide whether a delivery partner is worth a conversation.

01Who exactly is on the other end

CrewNexa, an agency rather than one person, and we are saying that up front rather than letting you find out later. Thirteen years building dedicated engineering teams for US, European, Australian and Middle East clients, run to ISO 27001 practices. It is the reason the capacity above flexes with the load instead of holding a seat. A named lead runs your queue, with cover behind them, so nothing stalls when one person is away.

02Security and hosting maintenance

Wordfence for the application firewall, malware scanning and login hardening. Cloudflare in front for DNS, TLS, caching, WAF rules and rate limiting. Hosting with daily off site backups, isolated staging and PHP version discipline. Core, theme and plugin updates go to staging and get tested before production, because most WordPress incidents arrive through an unpatched plugin rather than a breached password. Our own operations run round the clock network and security monitoring, so a restore is rehearsed before it is needed, not during.

03Where AI sits in the build

AI does the volume work, which is exactly where your margin lives. First draft copy against a brief, page variants across service and location, schema and alt text generation, internal link mapping, content refresh cycles and the classification step in the console above. We also ship LLM systems to production for clients, agents and retrieval pipelines on OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini APIs, so this is delivery experience rather than enthusiasm. It does not decide layout and it does not get the final word on anything a regulator reads. The compliance decisions on the clinic page were made by a person and checked twice.

04Testing, and how QA improves

A gate, not a habit. Nothing publishes until it passes, and you saw the list run above. Rendering at 360, 768, 1024 and 1440. Keyboard only navigation through every control. Contrast measured rather than eyeballed, which is how the contrast problem in your own palette was caught. Forms tested empty, invalid and submitted twice. Links and anchors resolved. Schema validated. Console clean. Reduced motion honoured. The same Playwright discipline that tests a thirty million user platform checked this page before you opened it, and every issue a client ever reports becomes a new line on the list, so the same thing cannot reach them twice.
